The function-space gap  g = f_A(x) + f_B(x) - f_AB(x)  (A, B are two domain experts/adapters; f_AB is their joint/composed behavior) -- which the entire model-merging field (TIES, DARE, Model Soups) treats as ERROR to MINIMIZE -- is actually the ROUTING SIGNAL. Gap magnitude predicts router calibration: calibration_steps is proportional to 1 / gap_magnitude. Orthogonal experts (cos ~ 0) produce a MAXIMAL gap -> fastest router calibration; correlated experts (cos >= 0.5) produce a small gap -> slow or impossible calibration.
